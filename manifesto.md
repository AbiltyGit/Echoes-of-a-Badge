# Artist's Manifesto

## Why this medium?
The choice of an interactive web application built with Streamlit was born from the desire to create a fast, accessible, and deeply personal space. A web form acts as a modern-day confessional. By reducing the visual noise and utilizing a dark, somber theme, the interface strips away modern distractions and places the user in a solitary headspace, mimicking the isolated, introspective moments characteristic of Bob Dylan's lyrical delivery. 

## What caught you?
What resonated most about "Knockin' on Heaven's Door" is the concept of surrender—taking off a "badge" that one can no longer use. It speaks to a moment of profound transition. The 1970s was an era defined by letting go of old paradigms and facing an uncertain future. This project captures that essence by asking users to identify their own "badge," their own burdens or past identities, and surrender them to the machine, generating a therapeutic farewell.

## AI as what?
In this creative process, AI was a **translator**. The human specifies a raw, unfiltered emotion, and the first AI (the LLM) translates that into the poetic vernacular of 1973. The second AI (the image generator) then acts as a **mirror**, reflecting the textual metaphor into a visual manifestation. Rather than replacing the artist, the AI bridges the gap between the user's emotional state and the historical aesthetic of the song, effectively becoming a collaborative partner in mapping human nostalgia.

## Your door:
"Knocking on heaven's door" represents crossing a threshold. It is not necessarily about mortality in the literal sense, but the mortality of our active phases—the death of innocence, the end of a relationship, or the graduation from one stage of life to another. By building this digital doorway, I am creating a ritual space for transitions. Every time the user interacts with the app, they leave something behind on the threshold, and DALL-E paints the space they are stepping into.
